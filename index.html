02 · Privacy filter

Clear head-on, dark from the side.

A privacy filter narrows the angle the screen can be read from. Light leaving the screen straight ahead gets through. Light leaving at a steep angle is blocked.

03 · Open back

Charge it with the case on.

The back stays open, so your charger connects as usual and the sensors stay against your wrist.
