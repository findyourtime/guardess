1. Who is responsible

Guardess is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Our legal name and address will be added here once our business registration is complete. For any question about your data, write to guardess.contact@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

When you browse this website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Fonts and scripts are served from our own site, not from third-party services. Like any website, our host (GitHub Pages) receives technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse.

When you use the shop

The shop section loads a Shopify script. It stores an identifier for your cart in your browser's local storage so that your cart is kept between visits. When you check out, Shopify collects the information needed to process your order: your name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number if you give it, the items you buy, and your payment details. Payment details are handled by Shopify and its payment providers; Guardess never sees your full card number.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and the content of your message, which we use only to answer you and handle your request.

3. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract: processing and delivering your order, handling returns and guarantees.
  • Legal obligation: keeping invoices and accounting records.
  • Legitimate interest: answering your messages, keeping the website secure, preventing fraud.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who receives your data

  • Shopify, which runs our shop and checkout, and its payment providers.
  • Shipping carriers, who receive your name, address and phone number to deliver your parcel.
  • GitHub, Inc., which hosts this website.
  • Google, which provides our email service (Gmail).

Some of these providers are based in the United States or other countries outside the European Union. Where this happens, transfers rely on safeguards recognised by EU law, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Order and invoice records: 10 years, as required by French accounting law.
  • Emails with customers: up to 3 years after our last exchange.
  • The cart identifier in your browser: until you clear your browser data or complete your order.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to receive a copy of it, to restrict or object to its use, at any time, by writing to guardess.contact@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

If you live in the European Union, you can also complain to your data protection authority; in France this is the CNIL (cnil.fr). If you live in California, you have similar rights under the CCPA, and we do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context advertising. If you live in Australia, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

7. Security

This website is served over HTTPS. Payments are processed by Shopify on a PCI DSS-compliant checkout. We only keep the data we need, for as long as we need it.

8. Children

Our shop is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when our business registration is complete or if we add new services. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
